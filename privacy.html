Privacy Policy

LogCatcher — Android logcat viewer for macOS and Windows.

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Short version. LogCatcher does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. There is no account, no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. Everything you see in the app stays on your own Mac or PC.

1. What LogCatcher does

LogCatcher is a desktop viewer for Android's logcat stream. It reads diagnostic log output from Android devices that you connect to your computer (via USB or a local adb server) and displays it in a filterable, searchable window.

2. What LogCatcher does NOT do

  • It does not require an account or sign-in.
  • It does not send any data over the network to the developer, to Apple, to Google, or to any third party.
  • It includes no analytics SDKs, no telemetry frameworks, no advertising libraries, and no crash reporting services.
  • It does not read any information from your computer outside of its own preference files (listed below) and the logcat stream from a device you explicitly connect.

3. Information LogCatcher reads

LogCatcher reads two kinds of information, both with your direct involvement:

  • Logcat output from an Android device you connect via USB or local adb. This is the device's own diagnostic log — the same data shown by adb logcat. It is shown in the LogCatcher window and never leaves your computer.
  • Local preference files that LogCatcher itself writes, so your settings persist across launches.

4. Local files LogCatcher writes

These files are stored on your own computer for your own use. They are never uploaded.

  • macOS
    • Preferences: ~/Library/Preferences/com.smallisbest.logcat.plist
    • Saved filters: ~/Library/Application Support/com.smallisbest.logcat/filters.json
  • Windows
    • Preferences: HKCU\Software\com.smallisbest.logcat
    • Saved filters: %APPDATA%\com.smallisbest.logcat\filters.json

You can delete these at any time; LogCatcher will start fresh on the next launch.

5. External tools

LogCatcher uses the Android Platform Tools' adb binary installed on your computer to communicate with connected devices. LogCatcher does not bundle, modify, or transmit adb. Communication with adb stays on your local machine (TCP loopback or a direct USB endpoint).

6. Children's privacy

LogCatcher is a developer tool aimed at adults. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing for children's data to be mixed with.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the “Last updated” date above will change. Because LogCatcher does not contact any server, you will not be notified automatically — please check this page if you want the current text.
